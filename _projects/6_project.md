Based on findings with the [WaveBot](/projects/8_project) and [FOSWEC](/projects/4_project), our team focused on a holistic design approach for wave energy converters, that could simultaneously factor in hydrodynamics, mechatronics, electronics, and control.
Multi-port modeling constructs, which are popular in RF and electronics fields, provide an excellent linear tools for understanding power flow and impedance matching in wave energy converters {% cite Bacelli:2021aa Coe:2024ac %}.
Along with nonlinear tools that can more fully capture complex dynamics, these tools allow for a "control co-design" approach to be applied to wave energy converters.
Our team has found immense value in apply these tools to a number of wave energy design problems {% cite Keow:2025aa Keow:2025ab Forbush:2025ab %}.
