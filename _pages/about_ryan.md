**Ryan Coe** received a B.S. degree in Ocean Engineering (2009) and a Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering (2013), both from Virginia Tech.
In 2013, Ryan joined Sandia National Laboratories' Water Power Technologies group where he served as a research engineer until 2026.
Ryan will be returning Virginia Tech in 2026 to become an Associate Professor.
Ryan’s research focuses primarily on modeling, testing, control, and design of ocean systems including wave energy converters (WECs), ocean vehicles, autonomous systems, and platforms.
Ryan is an Associate Editor for the _Journal of Waterway, Port, Coastal, and Ocean Engineering_, the _Journal of Offshore Mechanics and Arctic Engineering_, and the _Journal of Ocean Engineering and Marine Energy_ and an Editorial Board member for _Energies_.
