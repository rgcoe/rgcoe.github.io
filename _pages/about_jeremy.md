**Jeremy John** is a Master's student at Virginia Tech interested in ship design optimization and dynamics.
He received a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Virginia Tech in 2026.
Before beginning his graduate studies, Jeremy conducted submarine hydrodynamics research for Newport News Shipbuilding and applied gradient-based optimization to ship design research for Fincantieri Marine Group.
At the Virginia Tech National Security Institute, he worked on surface craft maneuvering, morphing-craft fluid dynamics, and environmental effects on ocean vehicle performance.
Jeremy also works closely with Ware Lab design team [Human Powered Submarine @ VT](https://hps.aoe.vt.edu/) for hydrodynamic design.
Jeremy's graduate studies are supported by the Department of Defense SMART Scholarship, and upon completion, he will join the Naval Surface Warfare Center, Carderock Division (NSWCCD).
