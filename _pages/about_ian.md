**Ian Hermanson** is a Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech focused on developing small-scale wave energy converters. He received a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Oregon State University in 2024. Before beginning his graduate studies in 2026, Ian worked at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory and the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. Ian's research interests include marine energy device design and testing, coastal community energy projects, and interdisciplinary engineering approaches.
