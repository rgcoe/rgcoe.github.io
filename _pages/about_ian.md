**Ian Hermanson** is a Ph.D. student at Virginia Tech focused on the development of small-scale wave energy converters. After graduating Oregon State University with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering in 2020, Ian worked at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory and the Pacific Northwest National Lab. Ian’s research interests include marine energy device design and testing, coastal community technical assistance, and interdisciplinary design methods.
